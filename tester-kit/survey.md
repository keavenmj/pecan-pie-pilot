# Pecan Pie end-of-week survey

Paste these into a Google Form, about ten minutes of setup. Each question notes the form question type and which item in the design doc's "What to measure" list it answers. The play logs already cover opens, scratches per session and retention, so the survey asks only what logs can't see: why people played, and how the reveal felt.

**Form settings:** turn off "Collect email addresses" (the tester code links answers to logs), allow one response, and don't require sign-in. It takes about 5 minutes to answer.

---

## About you

1. **What's your tester code?** It's at the bottom of the Collection screen, like T-4KZ9.
   *Short answer, required.* Links your answers to your play logs.

2. **What phone did you play on?**
   *Multiple choice:* iPhone / Android / Other

3. **Did you add Pecan Pie to your home screen?**
   *Multiple choice:* Yes / I tried, but it didn't work / No
   *Answers:* whether home-screen install matters for return visits.

## Overall

4. **How much did you enjoy playing Pecan Pie?**
   *Linear scale, 1 to 5:* Not at all → Loved it
   *Answers:* the core question, whether walking, scratching and collecting stick on their own.

5. **If Pecan Pie stayed available after this week, would you keep playing?**
   *Multiple choice:* Definitely not / Probably not / Not sure / Probably / Definitely

6. **What usually made you open the app?** Pick all that apply.
   *Checkboxes:* My daily card / A spot was nearby / I wanted to finish the set / I was out walking anyway / Just curious / Other
   *Answers:* which hook brings people back.

7. **On days you didn't play, what got in the way?**
   *Paragraph.*

## Walking and the map

8. **Did you ever walk somewhere specifically to reach a spot?**
   *Multiple choice:* Never / Once or twice / Several times / Most days
   *Answers:* whether nodes change where people walk.

9. **How sensible were the places spots appeared?**
   *Linear scale, 1 to 5:* Often odd or unreachable → Always made sense

10. **Was any spot somewhere unsafe, private, or impossible to reach? Where?**
    *Paragraph, optional.* Feeds node-placement fixes.

## The scratch

11. **How satisfying was scratching the panel?**
    *Linear scale, 1 to 5:* Tedious → Very satisfying

12. **The moment a card's values appeared usually felt…**
    *Multiple choice:* Exciting / Pleasant / Flat / Slow or annoying
    *Answers:* the doc's primary design question, whether the reveal carries real weight.

13. **Did you notice each card's condition (Pristine, Good, Fair, Poor)?**
    *Multiple choice:* Yes, and I cared about it / Yes, but I didn't care / I didn't notice it
    *Answers:* "Whether testers notice or care about condition grade."

14. **Did you ever scratch more slowly or carefully because you wanted a better condition?**
    *Multiple choice:* Never / Sometimes / Often / Every time
    *Answers:* "Whether testers slow down and scratch carefully on cards they say they care about." Compare with logged scratch time by rank.

15. **Did you find you could hold the panel to read through leftover scratch marks?**
    *Multiple choice:* Yes / No / I never had leftover marks

16. **Did leftover scratch marks on your cards bother you?**
    *Linear scale, 1 to 5:* Not at all → A lot

## What's under the panel

17. **Did you pull a Holo card? How did it feel?**
    *Multiple choice:* I didn't get one / It didn't feel special / A little exciting / Very exciting
    *Answers:* "Reactions to holo pulls."

18. **Did you pay attention to print numbers (like 47/500)?**
    *Multiple choice:* No / I noticed them / A low number felt special
    *Answers:* "Reactions to low print numbers."

19. **Did you look at the four stats (Prob, Luck, Entr, Var)?**
    *Multiple choice:* Never / Glanced at them / Checked them often

20. **Did you notice a stat shown in gold (past its normal range)? How did it feel?**
    *Multiple choice:* Didn't see one / Saw one, didn't care / Liked it / Didn't understand it
    *Answers:* "Whether testers react to Chance Factors at all, especially broke-the-ceiling rolls." That decides whether the axis carries into production.
