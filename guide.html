Pecan Pie tester guide

A one-week test. Walk to nearby spots, scratch cards, and build a set. Nothing costs money.

Open Pecan Pie

Before you start

  1. Open the game on your phone in Safari on iPhone, or Chrome on Android.
  2. Add it to your home screen first, then play from that icon. It opens full screen, like an app. On iPhone, the home-screen version keeps its own collection, separate from the browser, so install before you collect anything.
  3. Allow location when it asks. Choose While Using the App. The game uses it to show nearby spots and check when you've reached one.

iPhone

Tap the Share button, scroll, and choose Add to Home Screen. In Chrome on iPhone, the Share button is in the address bar.

Android

Tap Install when the game offers it, or open Chrome's ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

How to play

  1. Find a spot. Card-shaped markers HL on the map are spots to scratch, usually at parks, public art, landmarks, cafés, or along footpaths. Each shows how far away it is.
  2. Walk to it. When you're within about 75 m, it turns gold and pulses HL and says In reach. If your GPS is rough (common indoors), step outside or wait a moment.
  3. Tap it, then Scratch. You'll see which card you got right away. Its rank shows as pips: one to four dots, plus a diamond for the rarest.
  4. Scratch the silver panel. Drag one finger across it, like a coin on a scratcher. Everything under the panel is decided the moment you pass the reveal point, not before.
  5. Come back later. A spot recharges about 2 hours after you use it. You also get one Daily card each day, no walking needed: tap the button at the top of the map.

What's under the panel

Every card you scratch goes into your collection. What varies is how good that copy is:

  • Finish: some copies are Holo, with a rainbow sheen.
  • Print number: most cards are numbered, like 47/500. Low numbers are rare.
  • Condition: set by how evenly you scratch, not how fast. Cover the whole panel in light, even passes for the best result.
  • Four stats (Prob, Luck, Entr, Var): each has a normal range. A stat that lands past its range shows in gold. That's a rare one.

Your collection

  • Set shows the 12 cards in Harbor Lights. Empty slots are dark until you find that card.
  • My cards shows every copy you own, duplicates included. Tap any card to look closely.
  • Changed your mind mid-scratch? Tap Back. The same card waits for you at that spot, and your scratching so far is saved.

Bingo

  • You have a bingo board under Collection › Bingo. Every card carries a bingo ball in its top corner, like G·17. Collect a card whose ball matches an open square and it's daubed automatically, even a duplicate, and even if you keep it sealed.
  • Spots on the map change as you get closer. Within about 200 m, a spot shows its bingo ball. A gold ring means that number is open somewhere on your board. Within about 100 m, a pulsing star means that exact square is open.
  • Your first row, first column and first diagonal each earn a die and a badge, and each one pays better than the last. More lines in the same direction don't pay again. Fill the whole board for a Mythic die.

Dice

  • Before you scratch, tap Add a die under the card. When the card resolves, the die rolls and its total is added to all four stats, which can push one past its range into gold. You can swap or remove a die until your first stroke.
  • Everyone has a basic die that recharges as you walk (about 1 km), or slowly while the app is open (about 2 hours), up to 3 charges a day. Its charge is the round die button at the bottom right of the map; tap it to see all your dice. They're also under Collection › Dice.
  • Find more at dice caches (die pins on the map), or win them from bingo. Better dice roll higher.

Stay safe

  • Spots are placed at public places and on paths, away from roads, but map data isn't perfect. Never enter private property, a road, or anywhere unsafe to reach one. Just skip it.
  • Look up while you walk. Don't play while driving or cycling.
  • GPS is less accurate indoors and between tall buildings. That's normal.

Your tester code and feedback

Your tester code is at the bottom of the Collection screen, like T-4KZ9. The survey and problem report fill it in for you, so we can match your answers to your play.

Play however you naturally would. There's no target, and playing less is useful information too.

Something wrong? Use Report a problem (also linked at the bottom of the Collection screen) any time during the week. It attaches your tester code and a few details about the game automatically.

At the end of the week, please fill in the end-of-week survey. It takes about 5 minutes, fills in your tester code for you, and saves as you go.

What we collect

The game records gameplay events, like spots reached, cards scratched, and how you scratched, with your random tester code and the date. It doesn't send your location coordinates or your name. Contact details are only sent if you choose to add them to a problem report. Your collection stays on your phone.
